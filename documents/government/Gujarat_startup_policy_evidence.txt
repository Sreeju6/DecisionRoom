EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India lists the Gujarat Industrial Policy-2020 scheme for assistance for StartUps/Innovation. The accessible policy summary includes an interest subsidy of 8% per annum for three years, subject to a maximum of Rs. 4 lakh per year, and a sustenance allowance of Rs. 10,000 per month for one year for eligible innovators.
