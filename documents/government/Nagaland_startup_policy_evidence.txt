EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India lists Nagaland Startup Policy 2019 under the Department of Industries & Commerce, Government of Nagaland. The policy includes 100% reimbursement of stamp duty, registration fee and conversion fee for the first eligible transaction, and Startup India also records capital assistance of up to Rs. 25 lakh for incubators.
