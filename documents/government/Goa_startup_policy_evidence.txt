EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India's July 2026 state-policy directory lists Goa Startup Policy 2025 for 2025-2028. The accessible Startup India Goa policy page also documents startup support such as self-certification, subsidized incubator seat costs, stamp-duty reimbursement, interest subvention and a one-time grant for startups with a promising MVP. The detailed incentive page currently available through Startup India is labelled Goa Startup Policy 2021-25, so the older incentive details should not be treated as evidence for the 2025 policy without checking the newer policy document.

CURRENT-POLICY CAUTION

The current policy name and period are recorded separately from historical benefit details. The Research Agent must not merge historical benefits into a current-policy claim unless the current policy document confirms them.
