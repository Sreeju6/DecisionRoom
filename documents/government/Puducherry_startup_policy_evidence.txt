EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India lists the Aspiring Puducherry - Innovations & Startup Policy 2019. The policy supports incubation through the Puducherry Startup Cell and Atal Innovation Mission, and defines eligible startups around innovation, technology/IP or scalable business models. The policy also provides self-certification support for recognized startups.
