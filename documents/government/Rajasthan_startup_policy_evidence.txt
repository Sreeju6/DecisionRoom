EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India's 2026 Rajasthan State Report states that the Rajasthan Startup Policy 2022 is implemented through iStart Rajasthan. It provides ideation grants up to Rs. 2.4 lakh, with additional support for women-led startups, viability grants for seed-stage startups, scale-up funding, and a Startup Equity Fund with matching investment up to Rs. 5 crore per startup. It also describes reimbursement/subsidy support for patents, marketing and quality certification.
