EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India lists Odisha Startup Policy 2016-2020 and the current directory identifies the policy as Odisha Startup Policy 2016 with amendments, 2018-present. The accessible policy summary includes a monthly sustenance allowance of Rs. 20,000 for one year for eligible recognized startups, government procurement provisions including a 20% procurement target from Micro & Small Enterprises or Startups, and incubator capital support up to Rs. 1 crore.
