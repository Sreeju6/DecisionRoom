EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India's July 2026 policy directory lists Punjab Startup Policy 2026 for 2026-2031. The detailed Startup India policy page currently accessible is the older Industrial & Business Development Policy 2017-2022; it records historical startup support including incubator capital grants, interest subsidy, lease-rental subsidy and seed funding. These older details are explicitly marked historical and are not treated as benefits of the 2026 policy.
