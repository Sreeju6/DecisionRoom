Startup Chhattisgarh is a transformative initiative by the Department of Commerce &
Industries, Government of Chhattisgarh, designed to position the state as a dynamic center
for
innovation and entrepreneurship. Startup Chhattisgarh has emerged as a dynamic initiative to
nurture entrepreneurial talent and promote innovation across the state. Under startup
package
2024-2030 the state aspires to support startups at every stage of their journey from
ideation to
scaling and also support incubators, academic institutions and other stakeholders through
various incentives and capacity development.
Chhattisgarh Innovation and Startup Promotion Policy (2025-30)
The state provides support to startups at different stages of life cycle. Sector agnostic
schemes
such as, Student Start-up and Innovation Policy provides support to start-ups at idea stage,
whereas
Chhattisgarh Innovation and Startup Promotion Policy aims for assistance for Startups/
Innovation
provides support to start-ups at seed funding and scale up stage.

Chhattisgarh Innovation and Startup Promotion Policy (2025-30)
The state provides support to startups at different stages of life cycle. Sector agnostic schemes
such
as, Student Start-up and Innovation Policy provides support to start-ups at idea stage, whereas
Chhattisgarh Innovation and Startup Promotion Policy aims for assistance for Startups/ Innovation
provides support to start-ups at seed funding and scale up stage.
Through this policy, the state envisions empowering its youth, harnessing local talent, and
encouraging
risk-taking and innovation at every level. By fostering collaboration between academia, industry,
investors, and government agencies, Chhattisgarh aims to cultivate a self-sustaining startup
ecosystem
that generates employment, boosts competitiveness, and contributes to the vision of “Atmanirbhar
Bharat”.
This Startup Policy thus serves as a cornerstone for Chhattisgarh’s journey towards becoming a
knowledge-driven, innovation-led economy, ensuring that entrepreneurial spirit thrives at the
grassroots
and transforms the state into a powerhouse of ideas, enterprises, and opportunities.
Vision
To transform Chhattisgarh's startup ecosystem into a promising epicenter of innovation and
entrepreneurship, empowering local talent and inclusive economic growth.
Mission
Cultivating a vibrant culture of innovation and
entrepreneurship
Accelerating futuristic, social, and sustainable
solutions
Developing cutting edge technology infrastructure
Nurturing globally competitive skilled human force
Fostering impactful collaborations across sectors &
geographies
Operative Period
2025 - 2030
Scheme Eligibility
Recognized Startups, Incubators, Academic Institutions, Investors and Mentors
Available Assistance
Seed Funds, Interest Subsidy, Risk Free Loans and others

Startup Policy Guidelines
An entity shall be considered as a Startup
Up to a period of ten years from the date of incorporation/ registration, if it is
incorporated as a private
limited company (as defined in the Companies Act, 2013) or registered as a partnership
firm
(registered
under section 59 of the Partnership Act, 1932) or a limited liability partnership (under
the
Limited Liability
Partnership Act, 2008) in India.
Turnover of the entity for any of the financial years since incorporation/ registration
has
not exceeded one
hundred crore rupees.
Entity is working towards innovation, development or improvement of products or
processes or
services,
or if it is a scalable business model with a high potential of employment generation or
wealth creation.
Provided that an entity formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business
shall not
be considered a ‘Startup’.
Eligibility
To obtain State Startup recognition, an entity must fulfill the following conditions
An entity shall satisfy the definition of a 'Startup' as outlined in Clause 5 (1) of this Policy, or as per the latest applicable definition issued by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India, including any subsequent revisions or amendments there to; and
The entity shall hold a valid startup recognition certificate issued by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India; and
The entity shall have its registered office and having operations in Chhattisgarh to avail any benefits under this policy; and
The entity should not fall under the list of ineligible industries as per Annexure-3 of the Industrial Development Policy 2024-2030 issued by the Commerce and Industries Department, Government of Chhattisgarh.
Startups operating in sectors or activities that are limited to replication of already established conventional business units shall not be eligible for benefits under this policy
A Startup must register itself with Startup Chhattisgarh to attain full benefit of the Chhattisgarh Innovation
and Startup Promotion Policy
2025-30.

National Events
Reimbursement of 50% of the expenditure for participating in startup-focused events within the country, subject to a maximum limit of ₹2,00,000 per year. This support shall be extended for up to three events annually, with the cumulative reimbursement not exceeding ₹2,00,000.
International Events
Reimbursement of 50% of the expenditure for participating in startup-focused events outside the country, subject to a maximum limit of ₹3,00,000 per year. This support shall be extended for up to three events annually, with the cumulative reimbursement not exceeding ₹3,00,000.
Definition of Startup-Focused Event
A Startup-Focused Event shall refer to events organized with the objective of providing startups access to market opportunities, funding avenues, networking platforms, and promoting the startup ecosystem.

Government Incubator
Tier 1
Tier 1 Government Incubator
The proposed new government incubator shall be provided a grant of 75% of their project cost, out of which the maximum limit for capital expenditure (Capex) shall be 40%. The eligible operational expenditure shall be distributed over a period of five years and released on an annual basis.
Eligibility Criteria:
The proposed incubator must be registered as a legal entity in accordance with Clause 5. (2) (i) of this Chhattisgarh Innovation and Startup Promotion Policy
The proposed incubator must have a minimum of 3 years of operational experience and should have conducted at least 3 incubation cohorts.
It must have functioned as an incubator/accelerator for a minimum of 30 incubates and should have facilitated funding support to at least 4 startup units
Or
The proposed incubator shall be required to establish a formal collaboration or partnership with another incubator that meets the above-mentioned eligibility criteria.
The proposed incubator must have identified a minimum built-up area of 10,000 square feet within the state, with adequate facilities to accommodate at least 25 incubates.
Tier 2
Tier 2 Government Incubator
The Proposed incubators shall be provided a grant equivalent to 75% of their project cost, with the maximum limit for capital expenditure (Capex) capped at 40%. The eligible operational expenditure shall be spread over a period of five years and disbursed on an annual basis.
Eligibility Criteria:
The proposed incubator must be registered as a legal entity in accordance with Clause 5.(2) (i) of this policy
The incubator must have a minimum of 3 years of operational experience and should have conducted at least 3 incubation cohorts.
The incubator must have functioned as an incubator/accelerator for a minimum of 30 incubates and should have facilitated funding support to at least 2 startup units
Or
The proposed incubator shall be required to establish a formal collaboration or partnership with another incubator that meets the above-mentioned eligibility criteria.
The proposed incubator must have identified a minimum built-up area of 5,000 square feet within the state, with adequate facilities to accommodate at least 15 incubates
Upgrade
Upgradation Package
Incubators established in the state will be eligible for the grant mentioned in Clause 972 of the policy if they increase their approved fixed capital investment by a minimum of 25% and increase their incubation capacity (number of seats) by 25%.
Eligibility Criteria:
Must be an incubator as defined under Clause 5.(2)(i) of the policy.
Must have a minimum of 2 years of operational experience at the time of application.
The incubation center must have a seating capacity for at least 20 incubates and should have provided incubation to a minimum of 5 startup units.
Audited annual financial statements for previous financial years.
A qualified team must be available for mentorship, technical, and business support.
The incubation center must be a recognized incubator under any scheme of the Central/State.
Any additional criteria may be decided by the Nodal Department

Event Organization Assistance
Recognized incubators based in Chhattisgarh shall be eligible for financial assistance of ₹1 lakh per
event for organizing state -level programs related to startups, innovation, and entrepreneurship. This support is subject to a maximum annual limit of ₹4 lakhs per incubator, and the assistance provided
shall not exceed the actual contribution made by the incubator towards the event.
Private Incubator
Tier 1
Tier 1 Private Incubator
The proposed incubators will be provided a grant of 50% of their project cost, subject to a maximum limit of ₹3 crore. Within this, the maximum limit for capital expenditure (Capex) will be 40%. The eligible operational expenditure (Opex) will be distributed over a period of 5 years and disbursed on an annual basis.
Eligibility Criteria:
The proposed incubator must be registered as a legal entity in accordance with Clause 5.(2) (i) of this policy
The proposed incubator must have a minimum of 3 years of operational experience as an incubator.
The proposed incubator must have conducted at least 5 incubation cohorts and must have worked as an incubator for a minimum of 50 incubates and as an accelerator for at least 10 startups.
The incubator must have facilitated funding support for at least 5 startups in last 3 years through government schemes, angel funds, or venture capital
Or
The proposed incubator shall be required to establish a formal collaboration or partnership with another incubator that meets the above-mentioned eligibility criteria.
The proposed incubator must have identified a minimum built-up area of 10,000 square feet within the state, with adequate facilities to accommodate at least 25 incubates.
Tier 2
Tier 2 Private Incubator
The proposed incubators will be provided a grant of 50% of their project cost, subject to a maximum limit of ₹2 crore. Within this, the maximum limit for capital expenditure (Capex) will be 40%. The eligible operational expenditure (Opex) will be distributed over a period of 5 years and disbursed on an annual basis.
Eligibility Criteria:
The proposed incubator must be registered as a legal entity in accordance with Clause 5. (2) (i) of this policy
The incubator must have a minimum of 3 years of operational experience as an incubator.
The incubator must have conducted at least 3 incubation cohorts and must have worked with 30 incubates as an incubator.
The Incubator must have supported at least 2 startups in securing investments through government schemes, angel funds, or venture capital in the past three years
or
The proposed incubator shall be required to establish a formal collaboration or partnership with another incubator that meets the above-mentioned eligibility criteria.
The proposed incubator must have identified a minimum built-up area of 5,000 square feet within the state, with adequate facilities to accommodate at least 15 incubates. The proposed incubator must be registered as a legal entity in accordance with Clause 7.3 of this policy
Upgrade
Upgradation Assistance to Incubators
Incubators established in the state will be eligible for the grant mentioned in Clause 7.2 of this Chhattisgarh Innovation and Startup promotion Policy if they increase their approved fixed capital investment by a minimum of 25% and increase their incubation capacity (number of seats) by 25%.
Eligibility Criteria:
Must be an incubator as defined under Clause 5.(2)(i) of the policy.
Must have a minimum of 2 years of operational experience at the time of application.
The incubation center must have a seating capacity for at least 20 incubates and should have provided incubation to a minimum of 5 startup units.
Audited annual financial statements for previous financial years.
A qualified team must be available for mentorship, technical, and business support.
The incubation center must be a recognized incubator under any scheme of the Central/State.
Any additional criteria may be decided by the Nodal Department
Event
Event Organization Assistance to Incubators
Recognized incubators based in Chhattisgarh shall be eligible for financial assistance of ₹1 lakh per
event for organizing state-level programs related to startups, innovation, and entrepreneurship. This support is subject to a maximum annual limit of ₹4 lakhs per incubator, and the assistance provided
shall not exceed the actual contribution made by the incubator towards the event.
Accelerate
Acceleration Program Assistance
Incubation centers will be given reimbursement of expenses up to a maximum of ₹15 lakh for the event if they provide support to startups in the development phase through incubation centers, for organizing an acceleration program (cohort) for a minimum duration of 8 weeks.
Eligibility Criteria:
For this purpose, incubation centers must obtain prior approval from the nodal department, and the funds will be distributed only based on actual expenditure and achievement of approved targets.
Only incubators recognized by the state government will be eligible for this grant.
The proposed program must be at least 8 weeks long, include a minimum of 10 development stage startups, have a fixed curriculum, and have defined outcomes.

Mentors provide a sounding board for strategic decisions and offer crucial emotional support through the

Innovation On Campus
College Innovation Startup Cells (CISC)
Each eligible College Innovation Startup Cell (CISC) shall be provided with operational support of up to ₹5
lakhs per year, aimed at facilitating activities such as ideation, prototyping, mentoring, startup outreach, and
innovation challenges.
Eligibility Criteria:
The CISC must be established within a state/central Government affiliate or recognized educational institutes (Higher/Technical) located in Chhattisgarh.
The institution must have a functional innovation or entrepreneurship development cell or demonstrate commitment to startup promotion through prior activities.
The CISC must submit a yearly action plan outlining proposed activities, budget utilization, and expected outcomes.
The institution must commit to matching the operational support through its own resources or partnerships.
The CISC must maintain records of student participation, startup outcomes, and submit biannual progress reports to the Implementing Agency.
