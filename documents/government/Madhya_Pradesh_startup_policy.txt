FAQ
What qualifies as a “Startup” under the MP Startup Policy 2022?
“Startup” means an entity which is recognized by Startup India under the Government of India, Ministry of Commerce and Industry Department of Industry Promotion and Internal Trade, and is established and registered in the State of Madhya Pradesh. Basically, an entity holding a valid DPIIT recogination number and certificate. To apply for DPIIT recognistion, please visit, www.startupindia.gov.in
How can Startup login to MP Startup Portal
Startup who are recognised by Startup India, DPIIT, GoI can login in MP Startup Poral by using the same username and password what they have used for login on Startup India Portal.
How does a Startup obtain benefits under the MP Startup Policy 2022?
Startups who have successfully loggedin to MP startup portal can apply on for financial assistance on the Portal itself, with all the relevant documents.
