Startup India lists the Mizoram Entrepreneurship & Startup Policy, 2019. The policy summary states that entrepreneurship and startup awareness programmes are to be conducted across district headquarters and subsequently block and village levels, reflecting the policy's emphasis on building an early-stage entrepreneurship ecosystem.
