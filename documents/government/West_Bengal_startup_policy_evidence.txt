EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India identifies the West Bengal Startup Policy as effective through December 2021. The accessible policy summary records mentoring assistance, infrastructure/capital assistance for entrepreneurship development centres, and patent incentives for eligible incubated startups. Because the listed policy period has ended, these should be treated as historical evidence rather than current benefits.
