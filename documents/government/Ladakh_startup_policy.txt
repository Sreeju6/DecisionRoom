About
Dr Amit has over 19 years of teaching and research experience. He is a National Team Member of Global Entrepreneurship Monitor (GEM) India and one of the co-authors of the GEM India Report from 2014 to 2023. He has also participated in a training of trainers’ program on ‘Business Simulation Teaching’, held at Innovative Learning Solutions University of Tennessee, USA. Dr Amit has presented his research at conferences in China and Malaysia. At EDII, he leads Devbhoomi Udyamita Yojana supported by the Higher Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. This Yojana aims to develop student entrepreneurs in the state.
