The State of big Opportunity
Haryana is well known for its entrepreneurial identity owing to which, it has emerged as one of the most sought-after startup destinations in the country. Gurugram is the hub of Startup ecosystem and there are currently approximately 12000+ DPIIT registered startups in the State which are actively working in various fields i.e., in Information Technology, Engineering, Electronics, Automobiles, Textiles, and other Hi-Technology Industries.
