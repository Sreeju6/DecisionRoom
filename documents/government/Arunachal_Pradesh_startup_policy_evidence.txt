EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India lists the Arunachal Pradesh Startup Policy 2022-23. The policy provides annual entrepreneurship challenges, selecting 50 startups each year for five years; the top 10 can receive Rs. 5 lakh each with incubation and the next 40 Rs. 4 lakh each with pre-incubation. It also provides support for proof of concept, including support through the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme and state support for eligible startups not accommodated under that scheme.
