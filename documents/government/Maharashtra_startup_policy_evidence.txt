EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India's July 2026 policy directory lists the Maharashtra Startup, Entrepreneurship & Innovation Policy 2025 for 2025-2030. The accessible detailed Startup India page is the older Maharashtra Startup Policy 2018-2023; it records measures including self-certification, relaxed compliance norms, easier procurement, SGST-related support, stamp-duty/registration support and patent-cost assistance. These older details are retained only as historical evidence and must not be presented as current 2025-policy benefits without verification.
