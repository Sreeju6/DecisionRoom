EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India's July 2026 directory lists Tamil Nadu Startup and Innovation Policy 2023 for 2023-2032. The accessible detailed Startup India page is the older Tamil Nadu Startup & Innovation Policy 2018-2023. That page records one-stop startup registration/support, TANSIM services, IP support, procurement facilitation, a proposed Tamil Nadu Startup Fund of Funds of Rs. 250 crore, and nominal-lease land for startup parks. These details are historical unless verified against the 2023 policy document.
