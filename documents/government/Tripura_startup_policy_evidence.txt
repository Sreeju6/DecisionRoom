EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India's 2026 Tripura State Report states that the Tripura Startup Policy 2024 aims to position Tripura as an innovation and entrepreneurship hub in Northeast India by 2028. It focuses on incubation infrastructure, industry-academia collaboration, inclusive growth and innovation-driven enterprises. The report states that recognized startups can receive a one-time Tri-Seed Funding grant of Rs. 2 lakh at the idea stage.
