EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Startup India lists Bihar Startup Policy 2022-2027 under the Department of Industries. The policy includes self-certification support, exemption from certain licensing/registration requirements for five years subject to statutory safety licences, and other startup, incubator and investor incentives.
