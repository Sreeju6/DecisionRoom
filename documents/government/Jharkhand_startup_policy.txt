PREAMBLE 
Jharkhand as state exhibits robust business- friendly ecosystem, promoting innovation 
and nurturing entrepreneurial spirits. The government of Jharkhand is dedicated to craft 
opportunities for various stratums of the society by providing an equitable ecosystem for the 
development of entrepreneurship and innovation. 
The state through this policy is providing a hand holding approach for start-ups by 
enabling, empowering and engaging them from a very nascent stage and be the partner in 
their growth journey. The policy focuses on systematic effective pathway with short-term 
orientation to boost entrepreneurship, revitalize the economy, and investment in the state in 
coming 5 years and also paves a strategic long-term roadmap for inculcating and promoting 
the culture of entrepreneurship at individual as well as societal level. 
A. Policy Validity 
This policy will be valid for a period of 5 Years from the date of notification. 
B. Vision 
To be amongst the top ten leading startup States in the country by the year 2028, by 
providing an environment to "Entrepreneurs and Startups" for the creation of successful, 
innovative, and impactful enterprises through dedicated network of incubation centres, SEB 
andREI's . 
C. Mission 
Developing Entrepreneurship ecosystem to facilitate the growth and scaling of at least 
1000 new start-ups in coming next 5 years. 
D. Objectives 
(i) To create 1,00,000 sq. ft. area of dedicated incubation space offering end-to­
end support to the start-ups in the State, with specific focus towards tribal and 
rural community. 
(ii) To create a robust and synergetic platform for the Academia (04 incubation 
centres previously selected by SEB facilitating maximum innovative start-ups) 
along with more in STPI, Zila Udyog Kendra Vs ITis' Vs Electronic 
Manufacturing Cluster V s Various Industrial Development Authorities V s other 
options to attract tribal and rural community. 
(iii) To establish more incubation centres at industry & government administrative 
level across district with an intent to attract tribal and rural community. 
:: 1 :: 

(iv) To promote tribal, female, rural-ecopreneurs and specially-abled entrepreneurs 
in all possible areas of innovation at rural, semi-urban and urban areas across 
Jharkhand. 
(v) To support and promote one District One Product for the 14 districts of 
Jharkhand as identified under PMFME scheme of Government of India and 24 
Districts as identified by Industries Department, GoJ under the One -District -
One - Product scheme. 
(vi) To support young minds and talent from all comers of Jharkhand including 
schools, colleges, ITI's, universities, industries, communities, individuals, 
innovators etc. 
(vii) Developing and promoting pool of investors from Jharkhand, India or outside 
India (both public & private) to support start-ups on funding & internship 
opportunities.in Jharkhand. 
E. Focus Area's 
The key focus areas but not limited to for this policy are as below: 
(i) Tribal art, culture, language & tourism 
(ii) Tribal medicines & way of life 
(iii) Forest and Environment protection and rejuvenation 
(iv) Food Processing, Dairy, Exotic flowers, plants, vegetables etc. 
(v) Automation, Industrial and Technical Innovation 
(vi) Cyber Security and Information Technology 
(vii) Sports, Education, beauty products Sector 
(viii) Agriculture, Mining Sector 
(ix) Solid-Waste Management 
(x) Urban Infrastructure Planning & Environment 
(xi) Renewable Energy 
(xii) Creative ideas pertaining to sectors including and not limited to e-commerce, 
Digital Health, Logistics, Transportation, Warehouse and Cold storage, 
Automobile and auto parts, Green Vehicles, Rural innovation, E-Govemance, 
and other societal requirements. 
F. Centre's of Excellence 
This policy is meant to be largely sector agnostic, there will be a few sectors that will 
benefit from specific focused initiatives. The policy proposes setting up of 3 Centre's of 
Excellence (COE) to promote growth and traction in specific sectors of high potential. 
::2:: 

(i) Design 
There Is a strong sense of performing arts and artistry in the ethnic 
underpinnings of tribal culture of Jharkhand. Just leveraging it for crafts and 
performances will lead to a few small enterprises with no major aspirations to scale. 
At the same time design, and design thinking, have become core competencies of 
successful enterprises across the world. Also, there is huge growth in industries where 
there is a huge role of design, for example media, video gaming, digital art, animation 
etc. 
There should be a long term view to capitalize on and hone the artistic and 
design sensibilities of the tribal populace and, in the long term, promote Jharkhand as 
a centre of design and media. 
COE in Design will include an involvement from educational institutes with a 
track record in design and allied areas, as well as involvement from proven design 
firms. 
(ii) The 4th Industrial Revolution for core sector 
Jharkhand is blessed with natural resources and that has led to the setting up of 
several large enterprises in the core sectors. It is just not restricted to mining industries, 
but has led to several manufacturing and production industries as well. Also, there has 
been a mushrooming of several small and medium enterprises to services the 
requirements of these large industries. 
Some of the highest potential ofbenefit in the application of the 4th industrial 
revolution technologies lie in these traditional industries. Specifically, there is a huge 
opportunity for additive manufacturing (3D printing), automation/robotics and Internet 
of Things (loT). The COE should be set up with the involvement of major industrial 
entities of the state like Tata, NTPC and others. 
(iii) Agro/ Forest Products 
A large proportion of the state depends on agriculture and forest resources for 
their living. There is very little evidence of local value addition and processing. There 
need to be concerted efforts to involve startups working in these fields and create a 
network of allied services and products which lead to a high level of local value 
addition. 
The COE of agro and forest produce is to focus on creating implementable value 
chains in the state that help the populace take a larger role in the value chains and 
generate more value and wealth .

G. Components of Jharkhand Startup Policy 
The Government of Jharkhand proposes a two-pronged approach to create a robust 
startup -cum- entrepreneurial ecosystem under its Startup Policy. The first part of the policy 
proposes an Action Plan while the second part of the policy elucidates the various enablers 
that are required for creating successful startups as well as creating the larger entrepreneurial 
ecosystem in the State. 
One institution can apply only in one category in a year. The approved institution 
cannot avail similar benefit under state/central policy. Once approved institutions cannot 
apply /change/modify the category. 
1. Start-Up Support 
This action plan aims to offer support tailored to the need of every entrepreneur 
and the current stage of development of their Idea/venture. It shall provide one-on-one 
customized support covering a broad range of topics, services, and resources important 
to the growth of new startup companies through government/government-supported 
incubation centres and accelerators. 
1.1. Ideal Seed I Proto-type Stage- Support & Funding 
(i) Any individual or group of individuals with an innovative idea/concept can 
apply in any sector through the portal https://abvil.jharkhand.gov.in 
::4:: 

(ii) The selected individual or the group shall be provided with a stipend of INR 
15,000/- per group member (maximum 3) per month for 12 months as a 
subsistence allowance from the selection date. 
(iii) Jharkhand Females, SC/ST, Transgender or Divyang (differently-abled) eligible 
members from the selected individual or the group shall be eligible for an additional 
special allowance of INR 2000/- per month for 12 months over and above the 
subsistence allowance. 
(iv) During this duration, the selected individual/group has to work on agreed upon 
milestones 
a) Idea research, Idea development, and Concept validation 
b) Prototype development 
c) Business plan development 
(v) The selected idea/concept will be eligible for financial support upto INR 10 
lakh (excluding subsistence allowance and special allowance) for the 
development of the prototype/initial setup of the business/product/solution. 
1.2 Secondary stage or Scale-up Stage - Support and funding for the Startups 
(i) Selected start-ups who have achieved milestones (agreed upon at the Ideal Seed 
I Proto-type Stage) and show promise, shall be provided continued fund 
support to the start-ups. 
(ii) A one-time grant upto INR 10 lakh would be provided to start-ups for support of the 
business/product/solution for scale up their businesses. 
(iii) To be eligible for this stage, there should be gap of minimum three months from 
completion of the prototyping stage. 
(iv) The Government of Jharkhand can provide fmancial support to start-ups for 
scaling their product/solution and expansion of their businesses by its internal 
fund. 
(v) At the end of the programme, there will be a demo day where start-ups will be 
able to connect with financiers and other potential stakeholders to help them 
with the next stage of growth. 
(vi) All incentives listed below shall be applicable for a unit till the time it remains 
a 'startup' as defmed by Government of India.

1.3 Reimbursements 
(i) Reimbursement of GST paid: Start-ups, which are registered in Jharkhand, shall 
be eligible for reimbursement of 100% of NET SGST paid in cash by the unit 
per annum for 5 years. 
(ii) Patent Filing: The Government of Jharkhand intends to encourage the filing of 
patents by Start-ups located within the State. The Government therefore 
proposes to create a pool of resources for successful filing of patents. Colleges 
in the State of Jharkhand starting courses/ vocational courses for Patent Filing 
shall be reimbursed the cost ofiNR 25,000 per candidate passing out subject to 
a maximum of 20 candidates per year. This reimbursement shall be available to 
the colleges applying for the same. The State shall reimburse the cost of fee to 
filing patent a maximum ofiNR 50,000 per patent by the company/unit located 
in Jharkhand provided the patent is filed from Jharkhand office and granted. 
(iii) Registration fee/Stamp duty reimbursement: 100% reimbursement of stamp 
duty and Registration fee paid by Start-up on sale/lease deeds on the first 
transaction only. This incentive shall not be provided in case of Government 
land. 
(iv) Municipal duties: A three-year reimbursement on payment of all municipal 
duties, taxes and fees by the Start-ups. 
(v) Reimbursement on lease rentals: 75% of Lease rentals paid in the 1st year of 
establishment, 50% of the Lease rental paid in the 2nd year and 25% of Lease 
rentals paid in the 3rd, 4th and 5th years for office space, either in STPI, IT/ 
Multi-purpose SEZs/ IT Parks/ DTP or in any private areal location, subject to 
a maximum of INR 3 lakhs per annum, for a period of 5 years from the date of 
start of commercial production/operation . Above shall be enacted in light of 
Jharkhand Government Resolution no. - 48/Ra, dated 03.01.2017 and 
Departmental Resolution no. 4306/Ra, dated 24.10.2014. 
(vi) Internet Bandwidth Reimbursement:50% of Bandwidth connectivity paid to 
Internet Service Provider (ISP) by Start-up subject to a maximum of INR 
60,000 per annum, for a period of five years from the date of starting 
commercial production! operation. 
(vii) Electricity bills Reimbursement: A reimbursement of 30% of the amount paid 
to Electricity dis-corns, shall be reimbursed, up to INR 60000/annum on 
furnishing self-certified bills for a period of five years. 
(viii) Provision of Land in IT Parks/SEZ: Start-ups, which are registered in 
Jharkhand, will be prioritized for allocation of land/space in IT Parks and 
special economic zones (SEZ). Above shall be enacted in light of Jharkhand

Government Resolution no. - 48/Ra, dated 03.01.2017 and Departmental 
Resolution no. 4306/Ra, dated 24.10.2014. 
(ix) Govt. of Jharkhand intends to provide preference in tenders to start-ups as per 
Jharkhand Procurement Policy. 
(x) Reimbursement on Certification: The State Government shall reimburse, the 
cost incurred by a Start-up unit operating in the state up to a limit of INR 
50,0001-for domestic and INR 1.50 Lacs for international certifications per year 
on furnishing self- certified bills for a period of five years. 
(xi) Special incentives to SC/ST/Female/Transgender Divyang start-up: 
Additional 5% on lease rentals, internet bandwidth and electricity bills shall be

5 Human Capital Development 
It's imperative for the state of Jharkhand to create a vibrant culture of entrepreneurship 
and creativity for realizing the missions that are laid out in this policy. This requires that we 
reimagine and reinvigorate the role of universities and colleges as germinators, where young 
minds can sprout, out of the box solution and ideas. It's also vital that we promote 
entrepreneurial awareness, nurture skills and freethinking amongst citizen across age groups. 
To realize this, the Government has planned a strategic approach: 
(i) Short term Catalytic Interventions: Through idea conclaves, pitch your business 
ideas/concepts, organizing college startup fests/events etc. 
(ii) Medium term Interventions: To setup virtual entrepreneurial skills centre for 
improvement of soft skills, such as persistence, networking and self-confidence on the 
one hand and hard skills on the other hand, including basic startup knowledge, business 
planning, fmancial literacy, managerial skills etc. Government of Jharkhand will 
provide dedicated MOOC's (Massive Open Online Course) program (Self-Paced) 
developed in association with Higher education institutes/reputed training entities with 
certification (Optional). It will consist pre-recorded video sessions with lecture 
sessions, slides, videos, live successful stories, interviews, and MCQ sessions, for 
reaching out to masses. 
(iii) Long term Strategic Intervention: Through changes in the college/school curriculum, 
faculty training schemes, facilitating the use of startup kits in schools etc.

University/HEI's in the state to be facilitator to motivate the youths to take up

5.2 State funded Schools Syllabus 
The state school's curriculum will be updated to incorporate modules on basic 
electronics, entrepreneurship, digital and basic fmancialliteracy to develop an aptitude 
amongst the students. The Central Incubation Lab will provide knowledge assistance 
to the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand for the 
up-dation of the syllabus. All schools should focus towards the establishment of a 
Tinkering Lab. Knowledge support may be provided in association with Higher 
Educational Institutions. 
5.3 Faculty Up-gradation and training 
Faculties would undergo a tailor made training at the State's Central Innovation 
lab or other facility to develop a nuanced understanding of the latest development in 
the field of startups and other skills. Government shall also support (non fmancial) 
universities to train the faculty for promotion of innovation and entrepreneurship. 
Mentors to be invited from educational institutions, shortlisted for training programs 
or MOOC's certification to be offered for incubates. 
Training module can include (not restricted to): 
(i) Entrepreneurship module along 6C principle- Collaboration, Communication, 
Content, Critical thinking, Creative innovation and Confidence. 
(ii) Project management, business communication, manufacturing process, service 
delivery, customer relationship. 
(iii) Faculty Development Program - Universities/Colleges shall organize faculty 
development program to promote innovation and entrepreneurship at the college level. 
(iv) Establish E-Cells in Universities, Colleges and Schools - Entrepreneurship Cell 
(E-Cell) would be established to encourage and support college-level student to start 
their own Enterprise. 
5.4 Interest reimbursement for Student loans 
The student, whose ideas/concepts are to be selected by the State Evaluation 
Board for incubation in government I government funded incubation centres, shall get 
their interest reimbursed for study loans for a period of 3 years to a maximum of INR 
10 lakhs for each student, after graduating from the academic institution where they 
are pursuing their education. 
5.5 Distribution of STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering & Math's) kit in 
the schools 
Government would provide STEM kits in the government funded/private 
schools to help students learn basic computing, electronics and broaden their

5. 6 Idea Boot camps/Conclave 
Colleges and universities would be mandated to orgaruze quarterly boot

The Incubation centres shall organize business Idea/innovation/ challenges to

funded from the corpus maintained under Jharkhand entrepreneurship development 
fund. 
5.8 Scientific Conferences/Workshops for Academia-Industry cooperation 
An annual workshop is envisaged to foster close ties between the two. Eminent 
researchers and scientists will be called for the conference, which would also be

5.9 Weekend Business Challenge Programs 
Incubation Centre's in association with HEI's will organize startup weekends 
specially targeting Individuals who are in service or jobs. These events would facilitate 
their participation and help them to leverage their experience to enter into the realms 
of entrepreneurship. 
5.10 Special Platform for experience sharing 
Special platforms and tools would be created to tell your story, to have a voice 
and contribute to building a better company, community and state. It will also bring in 
mentors/alumni from the state, industry, academia & others, and give them a platform 
to share their experiences and learning. 
Domestic accommodation and travel expenses of all mentors of this purpose 
will be funded from the corpus maintained under Jharkhand entrepreneurship 
development fund.

Appendices Appendix-A 
(1) Definitions:-
(i) Innovation: The process of translating an idea or invention into a good or service that 
creates value for which customers will pay. Innovation involves deliberate application 
of information, imagination and initiative in deriving greater or different values from 
resources. 
(ii) Startup: Startup means an entity, incorporated or registered in Jharkhand: 
• Not prior to seven years, however for Biotechnology Startups not prior to ten 
years, 
• With annual turnover not exceeding INR 25 crores in any preceding financial 
year, and 
• Working towards innovation, development or improvement of products or 
processes or services, or if it is a scalable business model with a high potential 
of employment generation or wealth creation. 
Provided that such entity is not formed by splitting up, or reconstruction, of a business 
already in existence. 
Provided also that an entity shall cease to be a Startup if its turnover for the previous 
financial years has exceeded INR 25 crores or it has completed 7 years (for 
biotechnology startups 10 years) from the date of incorporation/ registration. 
Provided further that a startup shall be eligible for tax benefits only after it has obtained 
certification from the Inter-Ministerial Board, setup for such purpose. 
The entity accredited/certified as a Startup by Department of Industrial Policy & 
Promotion, Government of India, shall be considered as an eligible Startup for availing 
benefits under this policy only if the startup starts one or more of its business 
operations/ production facility in Jharkhand. 
Provided that a startup shall be eligible for tax benefits only after it has obtained 
certification from the "State Evaluation Board", setup for such purpose. 
Successful Startup: Startup would be considered successful once it raises series 'B' 
funding i.e. second round of fmancing by Private Equity Investors/self-sustainable 
business. 
(iii) Entity: 
Private Limited Company (under The Companies Act, 2013) or a Registered 
Partnership Firm (under The Indian Partnership Act, 1932) or Limited Liability 
Partnership (under The Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008). 
::21 :: 

(iv) Accelerator: 
Accelerator is an organization or program that enables rapid growth of new startups by 
providing specific services like: mentoring, training, resources and networking to help 
startups increase their customer base, generate more profits, prepare for funding and 
professionalize their organization. 
(v) Entrepreneurship: 
It's the act of being an entrepreneur, it implies the capacity and willingness to 
undertake conception, organization, and management of a productive new venture, 
accepting all attendant risks and seeks profit as a reward. 
(vi) State Evaluation Board: 
A special fix term board formed by the nodal agency for the evaluation and selection 
of the proposals received from Individuals, groups, and startups. 
(vii) Hybrid Institution: 
A hybrid institution is a combination of incubator and accelerator. 
(viii) Turnover: 
As defined under The Companies Act, 2013 
(ix) Female enterprise: 
An enterprise headed by female where 100% Stake is owned by female. Partial 
shareholding of female in an enterprise shall not be treated as female enterprise. 
(x) SOP and document checklist: 
Detailed Standard Operating Procedure and Document checklist would be issued separately.
